# MacScope Remote

## Short description

Monitor your Mac and run approved controls securely from Android.

## Full description

Keep an eye on your Mac and run approved actions from Android with MacScope Remote. Pair once with the MacScope app on your Mac to see live system health, browse redacted system details, review processes, and reach a focused library of captures and clipboard items.

LIVE MAC HEALTH

• Follow CPU, memory, network, storage, thermal, battery, and process activity
• Browse requested redacted snapshots without exposing an inbound port on your Mac
• Get optional alerts when your Mac needs attention

SAFE REMOTE CONTROL

• Search the utilities made available by the paired Mac
• Review the exact action and its risk before it runs
• Confirm sensitive actions with your device's secure authentication
• Use viewer, operator, and owner roles for the right level of access

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

• Live telemetry is forwarded while you watch and is not stored by the relay
• Commands expire quickly, are never queued while the Mac is offline, and must pass the Mac's local allowlist
• Pairing credentials stay in platform-protected secure storage
• Reset Remote on the Mac at any time to revoke every paired device

MacScope Remote requires the MacScope desktop app on an Apple-silicon Mac running macOS 14 or newer. Remote access is opt-in and must be enabled from the Mac.
